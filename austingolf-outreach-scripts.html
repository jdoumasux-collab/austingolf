Two approaches. Multiple scenarios. Use the words that feel natural — these are starting points, not scripts to read verbatim.

Approach 1 — Recommended for first outreach
The Free Trial First Approach

Lower barrier, faster yes. Lead with value before you ever mention price. This is the right call for the first 10 conversations — get partners in the door, prove the value, then convert.

Before you call — know these things

  • The course name and what makes it distinctive (you already know this from our directory)
  • Whether they're already on GolfNow (most are — it signals they understand online booking)
  • Ask for the general manager, head pro, or owner — not the starter or front desk
  • Best time to call: Tuesday–Thursday, 10am–2pm. Avoid Friday afternoons and Monday mornings
Phone Script — Your Version (Making the Call)
Cold Call · Free Trial Approach · ~4 minutes
When they answer: "Hi, is this [Name]? Great. This is [Your Name] — I own AustinGolf.com. Is now an okay time for about two minutes?" If yes, continue: "I'll keep it quick. I own AustinGolf.com — we're building the go-to online resource for Austin golf. We cover every course in the metro, with green fees, course guides, and booking links. GOLF Magazine actually named Austin the best golf city in America, and we're building the site that proves it. We're doing something a little different for our launch — I'd like to put [Course Name] in our directory with a full featured listing. No cost for the first 90 days. You get a professionally written course page, a booking link through GolfNow, and you show up every time someone searches for golf in your area. I'm not asking you for anything today except ten minutes of your time to get the details right. Can I send you our partner overview and schedule a quick call this week?" If they ask about cost after the trial: "After 90 days, featured placement runs $349 a month. But honestly — let's get through the trial first and I'll show you exactly what the traffic and clicks look like before we talk about that." If they say yes: "Perfect. What's the best email for you? I'll send the overview today and we can get your listing live this week." If they need to think about it: "Totally fair. How about I send you our partner overview and you take a look? No pressure either way — if it makes sense for [Course Name], great. If not, I appreciate you taking the call." Close every call with: "Thanks for your time. You'll hear from me by end of day."
Phone Script — Hired Rep Version
For a Sales Rep or VA Making Calls on Your Behalf
Opening: "Hi, I'm looking for the general manager or owner — I'm calling from AustinGolf.com about a partnership opportunity." When connected: "Hi [Name], my name is [Rep Name] and I'm calling on behalf of AustinGolf.com. We're the definitive Austin golf resource — 44 courses, green fees, booking links, the whole metro. We're reaching out to a select group of courses about our featured partner program. Here's the quick version: we'd like to feature [Course Name] on the site with a full course page, insider write-up, and direct booking link. The first 90 days are completely free — no strings attached. Our founder John Doumas has put together a partner overview I'd love to send to you. Is there an email address I can send that to, and would you be open to a 10-minute call with John this week to go over it?" Key things to convey: - We're not a national directory — we're Austin-specific - No payment for 90 days - The goal is a 10-minute follow-up call with the owner - This is a limited partnership program — not open to everyone If asked what it costs after the trial: "Featured placement runs $349 a month after the free trial — but John will walk you through all of that in the follow-up call. The first step is just seeing the partner overview."

What works on this call

  • Lead with the domain name — austingolf.com is the credibility
  • Keep it under 4 minutes
  • The only goal is the next step, not the close
  • Mention GOLF Magazine's "Best Golf City" quote
  • Always follow up same day with the email

What to avoid

  • Don't pitch the full pricing on the first call
  • Don't read this verbatim — make it yours
  • Don't call Friday afternoon or Monday morning
  • Don't leave a voicemail longer than 30 seconds
  • Don't follow up more than twice without a response
Approach 2 — For warmer relationships
The Direct Partnership Approach

Use this when you have a connection, a referral, or when a course has already expressed interest. Skips the free trial and positions it as a business partnership from the start. More appropriate for resorts, instructor programs, and non-endemic advertisers like auto dealers or luxury brands.

Phone Script — Your Version
Warm/Direct Call · Paid Approach · ~5 minutes
Opening: "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] — I own AustinGolf.com. [Mutual connection] suggested I reach out. Do you have a few minutes?" The pitch: "We're building the definitive Austin golf platform — every course in the metro, covered in depth, with booking links and editorial content. We just launched and we're being selective about our Featured Partners. I wanted to reach out about [Course Name] specifically because [specific reason — best par-3 in Austin, best value for the Bee Cave corridor, etc.]. We've written about your course in our directory already and the response has been strong. Our Featured Partner program puts your course at the top of every relevant search on our site, includes a full editorial article by our team, and a monthly spot in our newsletter. It runs $349 a month — and honestly, if you book two rounds from it in a month, it's paid for itself many times over. I'd like to send you our partner overview and schedule 15 minutes to show you exactly what the placement looks like. Does Thursday or Friday work for a quick call?" When they engage on price: "The $349 covers everything — the editorial, the newsletter placement, the booking link, and a monthly report showing your traffic and clicks. For context, a half-page in AvidGolfer runs $800-1,200 per issue and you get one issue. This is every month, with better targeting, and you can see exactly what's working." The close: "I'm keeping the initial partner program to about 15 courses — I want to make sure each partner gets real attention, not just a listing. If [Course Name] is a fit, I'd love to get you in. Can I send you the overview today?"

When they push back
Handling the Hard Questions

These are the real objections you'll hear. Read these before you call, not during.

We already advertise with AvidGolfer."
"That's great — they've been doing this for 25 years and they've earned it. We're different in one specific way: we're online-only, Austin-specific, and every visitor is actively searching for where to play right now. AvidGolfer readers get a magazine in their mailbox. Our readers are on their phone deciding where to tee off this weekend. The intent is completely different. And the first 90 days are free, so there's no reason not to try both."
We already have GolfNow. That's all we need for online booking."
"GolfNow is the booking platform — we're the discovery layer. When someone is deciding which Austin course to play, they're not starting on GolfNow. They're Googling 'best golf courses in Austin' or 'golf near downtown Austin.' We're what they find, and then we send them to GolfNow to book. We actually drive traffic to your GolfNow listing, not compete with it."
How much traffic do you have?"
"We just launched, so I'm going to be straight with you — traffic is building. That's exactly why the 90-day trial matters: I want to earn your confidence with real numbers before I ask you to pay for anything. By the end of the trial, I'll show you exactly how many people viewed your page and clicked through to book. If the numbers make sense, we talk about a paid partnership. If they don't, you've lost nothing."
We don't have a marketing budget right now."
"Perfect timing, then — the trial is free. No budget needed. Get your course listed, get the traffic data, and if it's working, the conversation about budget is easy. If it's not, you're out nothing. All I need is 15 minutes to get your details right."
What makes you different from every other golf directory?"
"Two things. First, the domain — austingolf.com is the most valuable Austin golf real estate on the internet. There's no competing with that in search. Second, we write about courses the way a local who's played them would write — not a database entry. Every listing has insider tips, seasonal advice, and honest assessments. That's what people trust, and it's what drives the clicks."
Can I think about it?"
"Absolutely. Can I ask what would make it an easy yes? I want to make sure I'm sending you the right information, not just the full overview. [Listen]. Got it — I'll send you [specific thing they mentioned] and follow up in a week. Does that work?"
We're a private club — we're not looking for new members."
"That makes sense, and honestly that's a different conversation. For private clubs, the value is visibility for your members and their guests — when someone visits Austin for a corporate trip and asks where to play, we want your name coming up. It's reputation and peer recommendation, not open enrollment. Let me show you what private club coverage looks like on the site — it might be different from what you're imagining."

Who We Are

Austin's Golf Home Base

AustinGolf.com is the definitive online resource for golf in the greater Austin metro. We cover every course — from $10 munis at Lions Municipal to Golf Digest Best-in-State resort tracks at Barton Creek. Every listing includes green fees, course specs, insider tips, and direct tee time booking.

GOLF Magazine named Austin the Best Golf City in America. We're the platform that proves it every week — through course guides, neighborhood breakdowns, visitor itineraries, and a free weekly newsletter read by the golfers who actually live and play here.

We're built on a simple belief: Austin golfers deserve a resource that knows the courses the way locals do — not the way a national platform pastes in a listing and moves on.

The Partnership Agreement

Plain Language.
No Surprises.

Thanks for considering a partnership with AustinGolf.com. We wrote this in plain language on purpose. If anything is unclear before you sign, ask us and we'll walk through it together. The short version: we promote your course honestly and effectively. You stay current on your listing details and your monthly partnership fee. Either of us can end it with 30 days notice. That's really it.

What we provide

We create and maintain your course listing on AustinGolf.com, including all course details, green fee ranges, specs, booking links, and photography. For Featured and Anchor partners, we write editorial content featuring your course and include you in our newsletter on the schedule we agree on. We send you a monthly report showing your page performance. We handle all technical hosting, SEO, and content updates on our end.

What we ask of you

Keep us current. If your green fees change, your course closes for renovation, or anything significant shifts — let us know within a week so we can keep your listing accurate for golfers. We'll reach out once a quarter to confirm details are still correct.

We also ask that you don't ask us to misrepresent your course. Our readers trust our editorial voice. We'll always be honest about what your course is great at — and that's more valuable to you long-term than any exaggerated claim.

The 90-day free trial

Every new partner starts with a 90-day free Featured listing. No payment information required upfront. At the end of 90 days, we'll share your performance report and have a quick conversation about whether a paid partnership makes sense. If it does, great. If it doesn't, no hard feelings — your basic listing stays live regardless.

Pricing and billing

Monthly partnership fees are billed on the 1st of each month. We accept all major credit cards and ACH bank transfer. If a payment doesn't process, we'll reach out before making any changes to your listing. We're not going to quietly downgrade you — we'll have a conversation first.

Editorial independence

Our editorial voice is not for sale. A partnership guarantees your course is featured prominently and accurately — it does not guarantee a positive review if your course has real problems, and it does not allow you to edit our written content. Partners who are great courses get great coverage. That's the model that makes our recommendations worth anything to readers, and therefore worth anything to you.

How long this lasts and how it ends

Partnerships run month-to-month after the 90-day trial and renew automatically. Either of us can end it with 30 days written notice — no reason required, no penalties, no hard feelings. When a partnership ends, your listing returns to a standard unpaid listing rather than disappearing entirely. We built this to be a long-term relationship, but we want you to stay because it's working, not because leaving is complicated.

A few standard things

Your pricing and promotions are entirely your own — anything we suggest for seasonal tie-ins is only a suggestion. Each of us is responsible for our own side: we stand behind the accuracy of what we write, and you're responsible for the experience golfers have when they arrive at your course. This agreement covers AustinGolf.com partnership placement only and is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.

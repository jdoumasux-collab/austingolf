Course Review · By Jake Mercer

Why Falconhead Is the Most Photographed Hole in Austin

There are 54 golf courses in the greater Austin metro. I've played most of them. But only one has a hole that makes people stop mid-round, pull out their phone, and forget they're supposed to be playing golf.

That's the par-3 8th at Falconhead. A carry over limestone falls to a green backed by cedar and live oak — it's the kind of shot that looks harder than it plays, and plays harder than it looks. At about 160 yards from the middle tees, it's manageable for most golfers. But the fear factor is real, and the photography opportunities are among the best in Texas.

Falconhead opened in 2003 as a PGA Tour Design Center project, and it shows. The routing is thoughtful — it uses the Hill Country terrain rather than fighting it. The front nine plays through relatively open land, setting you up for a back nine that drops into dramatic limestone canyons. The conditions are consistently maintained at a level most daily-fee courses can't match.

Green fees run $65-$120 depending on time and season. For the quality of the golf and the drama of the course, it's one of the better values in the Austin area above $50. Book early in the week for the best rates.
